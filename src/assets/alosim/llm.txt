## aloSIM Background 

aloSIM is a travel eSIM provider and app that lets users buy prepaid mobile data plans for travel anywhere in the world. Rather than using a physical SIM card, customers purchase an eSIM (embedded SIM) digitally and install it on their phone or tablet to connect to local mobile networks with data plans starting at low prices. The company was created by AffinityClick Inc. and aims to eliminate roaming fees and simplify travel connectivity

## Pricing Model

aloSIM offers three types of prepaid data plans with transparent pricing:

**1. Single-Country Plans**

Pricing based on:

– Specific destination country

– Data volume (typically ranging from 1GB to 20GB+)

– Validity period (number of days the plan remains active)

Entry-level pricing in high-demand destinations typically starts between $3.50–$6.00 USD, with most major markets offering plans starting at $4.50 USD.

**2. Regional Packages**

Multi-country coverage for travelers visiting multiple destinations within a region:

– Regional packages (e.g., Europe, Asia, North America, Caribbean) range from $5.00–$10.00

– Provide coverage across multiple countries at a single price point

– Offer greater value for multi-country trips compared to purchasing individual country plans

**3. Global Packages**

Worldwide coverage for international travelers visiting multiple continents:

– Global packages range from $10.00 (1GB) to $72.50 (Unlimited)

– Work in 200+ countries and destinations

– Ideal for business travelers and digital nomads with extensive travel itineraries

The majority of destinations (95 out of 224) offer entry plans under $5.00, making aloSIM highly competitive for budget-conscious travelers.
